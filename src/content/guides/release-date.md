**World of Warcraft: Forever launches on November 4, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PST.**

It's one global launch. No region gets it first. A **subscription or game time** covers access.
There is no box price.

Everything below comes from Blizzard's own pre-purchase announcement. Nothing here is a leak or a
countdown site.

## The hour, where you are

| Zone | Time |
|---|---|
| Pacific (PST) | Nov 4, 3:00 p.m. |
| Central (CST) | Nov 4, 5:00 p.m. |
| Eastern (EST) | Nov 4, 6:00 p.m. |
| UTC | Nov 4, 23:00 |
| UK (GMT) | Nov 4, 11:00 p.m. |
| Central Europe (CET) | Nov 5, 12:00 a.m. |
| Beijing | Nov 5, 7:00 a.m. |
| Sydney (AEDT) | Nov 5, 10:00 a.m. |

Blizzard states the time in PST. November 4 lands after the US clocks go back. Europe has switched by then
too. So these offsets are final, with no summer-time correction.

## What happens between now and then

All six dates below are from the same announcement.

| Date | What |
|---|---|
| Oct 20 | Invite-A-Friend codes start arriving by email |
| **Oct 21** | The global beta ends |
| Oct 27 – Nov 3 | Early Name Reservation: create and customise up to three characters |
| **Nov 4, 3 p.m. PST** | Launch |
| Nov 4 – Nov 11 | Launch week: invited friends can play without a subscription |
| Jan 11, 2027 | The Warcraft Forever Collection leaves the shop |

The last three dates are easy to get wrong.

The Collection is not a permanent edition. It retires in January. Invite codes are for launch week only,
and they do **not** include beta access. Name reservation isn't early access either: you build the
character, not the progress.

## Name reservation, in plain terms

- Runs **October 27 through November 3**.
- Opens **1:00 p.m. PDT on October 27** in North America. Europe opens at 18:00 CET.
- Korea and Taiwan open at 07:00 local time on October 28.
- You need the Digital Upgrade or Collector's Edition. The Skyborne Heroic Pack, Skyborne Epic Pack and Warcraft Forever Collection count.
- It only works on the WoW account that bought it.
- **Up to three characters in total**, across all rulesets. Two on PvP leaves one for Normal or Role-Play.
- Names are **first come, first served**. Blizzard says desired names cannot be guaranteed.

So the practical play is simple. Decide your race, class, name and look before October 27. Then be ready
that morning.

Still choosing? The [race × class matrix](/race-class/) has all 56 legal combinations. Every [race](/races/)
and [class](/classes/) page lists what that pick actually gets.

## Do you have to buy an upgrade?

No. A subscription or game time is the only thing you need to play on November 4.

The paid upgrades add cosmetics. Two of them also add things you cannot get later. Beta access is one, and
it expires on October 21. The other is 30 days of game time. Name reservation comes with any upgrade
purchase. Blizzard lists the current editions and regional prices on the Battle.net Shop.

here too. **1.60.1.70450** is the last build we have data for. Once the beta closes, there is nothing new
