The Forever Open is the first big community event on the Forever beta. Every competitor levels a **fresh
Horde character to 30**, then duels for a **$100,000** prize pool.

It is **not a Blizzard event**. It is run by **Tips Out, Esfand and Xaryu**, with sign-ups and check-in on
Raider.IO. Partners include Starforge Systems and RestedXP. The organizers' own event pages and Discord
have the final word on anything that changes.

## Schedule, in Central time

| Stage | When (CT) |
|---|---|
| Registration opened | Sept 27, 1:00 p.m. |
| Registration closed | Oct 2, 12:00 p.m. |
| Check-in | Oct 17, 9:00 – 10:00 a.m. |
| Qualifiers | Oct 17, 12:00 p.m. |
| Finals | Oct 18, time set by the organizers |

Raider.IO publishes the key times in Pacific, Central and Central European time. The finals start time was
still listed as TBD when this page was written. Check the organizers' channels on the day.

## The rules that shape the bracket

- **Level 30**, on the beta's **PvP ruleset**, **Horde** only.
- A **brand-new character** created on or after September 27, 1:00 p.m. CT. Older characters were
  removed from the event.
- Every character carries the surname **Foreverpvp** — Forever uses a two-name system, which is what
  makes a shared surname possible.
- Check in on Raider.IO the morning of the qualifiers, be level 30, and pay a **15 gold** entry fee.
- **Submit your talent build at check-in. It is locked.** No respeccing between opponents, so every
  build has to hold up against all nine classes.
- The **top four of each class** advance from the qualifiers into the finals bracket.

## The prize split

| Placement | Prize |
|---|---|
| Tournament champion | $50,000 |
| Top player of each of the nine classes | $5,555 each |

Ten races run at once. Even a class that goes out early still crowns a $5,555 winner.

Coverage of the announcement credits a $50,000 addition from TimTheTatman. The $100,000 total is the figure
on Raider.IO's event page.

## What 30 means in Forever

Talents start at level 10, and the original tier structure is intact. A level-30 character has **21 talent
points** to spend. That reaches the **16-point tier** comfortably. The **21-point tier** needs everything in
one tree. The 31st-point capstones are out of reach.

## What misses the bracket

Abilities that arrive late are simply not in play. That rules out **Frostfire Bolt** (40), **Shadow Word:
Death** (32), **Binding Heal** (32), **Conflagrate** (32), **Trueshot Aura** (32), **Mutilate** (40), **Lava
Burst** (40), **Summon Hawk** (36) and **Riptide** (50). The client puts the **Druid** Lacerate at 42 as well.

One correction, because coverage of this event repeats it: **Hunter Lacerate is available**. The Survival
talent's own ranks start at level 30. It is the Druid version that waits.

## Details a viewer can check

All of these come from the client's own spell text. Every one of them matters in a duel:

- **Sap no longer requires stealth and no longer breaks it.** Classic: "Must be stealthed." Forever:
  "Does not break Stealth." Combined with Cheap Shot it changes what an opening looks like.
- **Traps lost the out-of-combat restriction.** The Classic text for Immolation Trap ends with "Traps
  can only be placed when out of combat." The Forever text drops that line, and Frost Trap is a level-28
  trainer spell.
- **Fear Ward is a level-20 priest spell with a 3-minute duration** (Classic: 10 minutes). Every Horde
  priest race has it, so racial choice no longer decides who can break fear.
- **Priests have new low-level tools**: Confounding Flash (10, confuses up to five enemies), Divine Grace
  (10, heals below 50% and strips Weakened Soul, not usable on yourself), Chastise (20, immobilises for
  2 seconds).
- **Shamans drop four totems at once.** Call of the Elements (20) and Call of the Ancestors (30) place a
  full set, Totemic Recall (20) refunds 25% of their mana, and Totemic Projection (22) moves them.
- **Victory Rush** heals 10% of maximum health, but only within 20 seconds of killing a non-trivial enemy.
  In a duel nothing dies except a player, so it rarely fires.
- **Insect Swarm was toned down** on the way in: 138 damage over 12 seconds in Classic, 90 in Forever,
  with the 2% miss debuff unchanged. That is the Balance druid's 16-point tier.
- **Ice Block moved into the 16-point tier** of Frost, which is why mages can have it at 30.
